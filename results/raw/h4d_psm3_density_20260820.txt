--- notes ---
oshrun aborts with "could not find a launcher" on a bare VM. SOS's oshrun wraps whatever
launcher configure found, and there is none without Slurm or MPI installed. mpich supplies
mpiexec.hydra, which speaks PMI-1, which is what SOS bootstraps against.

Inter-node SSH: Rocky 8 with OS Login ignores ~/.ssh/authorized_keys. Add the launching
node's public key to the OS Login profile instead:
  gcloud compute os-login ssh-keys add --key-file=node.pub --ttl=6h
